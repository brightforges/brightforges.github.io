# Bright Forge — Modern Single-Page Company Website

A high-performance, responsive single-page company website built for **Bright Forge**, a premier digital engineering and product foundry.

> *"We Forge Ideas Into Impact."*

---

## 🎨 Brand Identity & Design System
- **Company Name:** Bright Forge
- **Theme:** Crafting and building resilient digital platforms from raw ideas.
- **Color Palette:**
  - Deep Navy: `#121A2B` (Primary Dark)
  - Orange: `#FF6A00` (Primary Accent)
  - Golden Yellow: `#FFC83D` (Highlight & Sparks)
  - White: `#FFFFFF` (Main Background)
  - Light Gray: `#F4F6FA` (Section Contrast Background)
- **Typography:**
  - Headings: `Montserrat` (Bold, confident)
  - Body: `Inter` (Legible, modern)
- **Logo:** Custom SVG anvil with glowing animated spark.

---

## 🏗️ Structure & Ordered Sections
1. **Sticky Navbar:** Brand logo, navigation links (*Home, About, Services, Portfolio, Testimonials, Contact*), orange "Get a Quote" modal trigger, and mobile hamburger drawer.
2. **Hero:** Navy canvas with interactive glowing spark particle physics, gradient glow, headline *"We Forge Ideas Into Impact."*, subheadline, *"Get Started"* & *"View Our Work"* CTA buttons, and credibility badges.
3. **About Us:** Foundry narrative, mission statement, engineering standards card, and **4 animated counters** (*Projects Delivered [350+], Happy Clients [180+], Years of Experience [14+], Team Members [52+]*) triggered on scroll via `IntersectionObserver`.
4. **Services:** 6 interactive cards with hover lift and orange glow (*Custom Software, Cloud Architecture & DevOps, Enterprise AI & ML, High-Performance Web Platforms, Product Strategy & UX/UI, Cybersecurity & Compliance*).
5. **Why Choose Us:** 4 battle-tested advantages on light gray `#F4F6FA` background (*Battle-Tested Precision, Rapid Iteration & Agility, End-to-End Partnership, Transparent Accountability*).
6. **Portfolio:** Responsive 6-project grid with realistic case studies, hover overlay, metadata tags, and interactive category filter tabs.
7. **Process:** 4-step horizontal timeline (*Discover, Design, Build, Launch*) with a glowing energized connector line and mobile-friendly vertical layout.
8. **Testimonials:** Interactive carousel with 3 client reviews, verified photos, star ratings, dot navigation, previous/next buttons, auto-play with pause-on-hover, and touch swipe gestures.
9. **Call-to-Action Banner:** Orange-to-gold gradient with high-contrast booking action.
10. **Contact:** Fully validated form (*Name, Email, Phone, Message*), direct company details, social links, and an interactive map placeholder with custom location beacon.
11. **Footer:** Brand emblem, foundry description, navigation links, service directory, and copyright notice `© 2026 Bright Forge. All rights reserved.`

---

## ⚡ UX Features Included
- **Interactive Particle System:** HTML5 Canvas generating embers and sparks that react gently to mouse movement.
- **Scroll Spy & Reveal:** Active navigation states and fade-in animations as sections scroll into view.
- **Quote Modal Dialog:** Pop-up blueprint request form accessible via the navbar and CTA buttons.
- **Form Validation & Toast Notification:** Client-side validation with real-time feedback and notification toast.
- **Back to Top Button:** Smoothly scrolls back to the hero section.
- **Direct Contact Channel:** Official single point of contact via `support@brightforges.in`.
- **Legal & Store Compliance:** Comprehensive Privacy Policy and Terms of Use tailored for Google Play Store, Apple App Store, and Paid Applications with explicit non-refundable digital goods disclosures.
- **SEO & Social Metadata:** Fully configured Open Graph, Twitter card tags, meta description, and SVG favicon.

   ```bash
   # Using VS Code Live Server extension, or any HTTP server:

   ```
