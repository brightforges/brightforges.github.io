Bright Forge Privacy Policy

Our commitment to protecting your digital footprint, securing enterprise credentials, and upholding strict non-disclosure across all forged architectures.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 Last Updated: November 2026 Paid Apps & Digital Goods Policy SOC 2 Type II Audited GDPR & CCPA Compliant

Privacy & Paid Apps Summary in Plain English

At Bright Forge, we do not sell your data. We do not monetize your inquiries, nor do we run invasive tracking inside our software. All transactions for our paid applications and digital software are processed through certified third-party app stores (Google Play, Apple App Store). Digital software purchases deliver instant access upon purchase and are strictly non-refundable once downloaded or activated. We protect all client code, user data, and system diagnostics with zero-trust security.

1 Introduction & Scope of Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to all services, consulting engagements, software development projects, web portals, and paid digital applications ("Paid Apps", "Applications", "Digital Products") operated and published by Bright Forge Inc. ("Bright Forge", "we", "us", or "our").

This policy governs the manner in which Bright Forge collects, uses, maintains, and protects information gathered from:

  • Visitors and users of our official website (brightforges.github.io).
  • Users who download, purchase, license, or install our Paid Applications and software tools distributed via the Google Play Store, Apple App Store, desktop channels, or GitHub.
  • Enterprise clients and partners utilizing our engineering and software development foundry.

By visiting our site, purchasing or downloading any of our Paid Apps, or entering into an agreement with Bright Forge, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this policy.

2 Information We Collect

We collect only the minimal information necessary to deliver, secure, and maintain our applications and engineering deliverables:

  • Direct Inquiries & Support Communications: When you contact our support desk at support@brightforges.in, submit a project blueprint inquiry, or report an application issue, we collect your name, email address, and message contents to assist you.
  • Paid Application Purchase & Entitlement Metadata: When you purchase one of our paid applications through certified third-party app stores (such as Google Play Store or Apple App Store), we receive an anonymized transaction confirmation token, purchase timestamp, product ID, and license entitlement status to unlock application features. We never receive or store your payment card numbers or bank credentials.
  • Application Performance & Crash Diagnostics: Anonymized technical telemetry including operating system version, device hardware model, application build version, and fatal error stack traces strictly required to diagnose crashes and deploy stability patches.
  • Client Project & Technical Data: When under commercial contract, we may handle architectural diagrams, code repositories, and configuration parameters strictly required to build and deploy custom client deliverables.

Paid Applications, Payment Processing & Non-Refundable Policy

Strict Digital Goods Commercial Disclosures

Bright Forge publishes premium, commercial software applications designed for high performance, reliability, and precision. Please review our mandatory terms regarding payments and refunds:

Zero Payment Card Data Retention

All billing and financial transactions for our Paid Apps, in-app purchases (IAP), and digital licenses are executed directly by certified third-party digital marketplaces and payment processors (e.g., Google Play In-App Billing, Apple App Store / StoreKit, or Stripe).

Bright Forge does not collect, view, process, or store your credit card numbers, debit card details, bank account numbers, or billing security codes (CVV/CVC). All payment details are processed under the respective platform operator’s PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure and privacy terms.

Strict Non-Refundable Policy — All Sales Are Final

All purchases of Bright Forge Paid Applications, in-app digital unlocks, feature enhancements, and digital license keys are strictly non-refundable and all sales are final once completed.

Because digital software applications, binaries, and digital licenses provide instant delivery and immediate access upon successful payment, the statutory right of withdrawal or cancellation is fully waived upon initiation of the download or license activation, to the maximum extent permitted by applicable international consumer law.

Non-Refundable Circumstances Include, but are not limited to:

  • Change of mind, buyer remorse, or accidental purchases made by the user.
  • Failure to verify hardware specifications, OS version compatibility, or memory requirements prior to purchase.
  • Inability to run the application due to rooted, jailbroken, or modified device operating systems.
  • Discontinuation of personal use or infrequent utilization of the software.
App Store Platform Exception: If your purchase was processed through the Google Play Store or Apple App Store, any refund claims are subject exclusively to the platform’s dispute mechanisms (such as Google Play’s automated refund window or Apple’s Report a Problem portal). Bright Forge does not have access to debit your store account or override store billing decisions.

No In-App Ads & Zero Behavioral Profiling

Our paid applications do not contain third-party advertising SDKs, ad trackers, or third-party behavioral profiling scripts. You pay for the software once; your device usage data is never sold, leased, or broadcast to data brokers.

4 Application Permissions & Local Data Storage

To deliver their core functionality, our Paid Applications may request access to select device capabilities:

Network Access

Used strictly to ping app store licensing APIs, verify active digital entitlements, and check for software stability updates.

Local Device Storage

User configuration files and project outputs are stored locally on your device hardware. Bright Forge never silently uploads or scans your local personal files.

You may manage or revoke permissions at any time through your device operating system settings (Android, iOS, macOS, Windows).

5 Client Code & Proprietary IP Isolation Guarantee

Our Strict "Zero Unauthorized Training" Policy:

Bright Forge guarantees that no client source code, proprietary algorithms, database schemas, or trade secrets are ever ingested into public artificial intelligence models, third-party LLM training pipelines, or shared repositories. Every client workspace is isolated within dedicated virtual private clouds (VPCs) with strict role-based access control (RBAC).

6 How We Utilize Your Information

We process collected data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Delivering, maintaining, validating licenses, and optimizing our Paid Applications.
  • Engineering, testing, deploying, and maintaining custom software deliverables.
  • Generating architectural estimates, formal project bids, and milestone updates.
  • Delivering critical security patches, emergency on-call notices, and stability alerts.
  • Fulfilling legal, tax, accounting, and compliance audit obligations.

7 Enterprise Security Infrastructure

We implement comprehensive defense-in-depth security measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure:

Cryptographic Encryption

All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3. Data at rest is encrypted via AES-256 with rotating KMS keys.

Zero-Trust Access Control

Hardware MFA tokens, ephemeral SSH credentials, and least-privilege policies enforced across all engineer accounts.

9 Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Bright Forge Paid Applications and web services are designed for general professional, developer, and business audiences. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 within the European Economic Area), nor do our paid applications target children.

If a parent or guardian discovers that their minor child has downloaded an application or transmitted personal information without consent, please notify us immediately at support@brightforges.in so that we may promptly remove any corresponding records.

10 Your Rights Under GDPR & CCPA/CPRA

Depending on your jurisdiction, you retain full statutory rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: You may request a complete export of personal information held by us.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request correction of outdated or inaccurate personal details.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request complete deletion of your records, subject to regulatory retention laws.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We do not withhold service or penalize any user exercising privacy rights.
