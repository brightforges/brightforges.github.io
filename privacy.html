Bright Forge Privacy Policy

Our commitment to protecting your digital footprint, securing enterprise credentials, and upholding strict non-disclosure across all forged architectures.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 Last Updated: November 2026 SOC 2 Type II Audited GDPR & CCPA Compliant

Privacy in Plain English

At Bright Forge, we do not sell your data. We do not monetize your inquiries. We treat all client code, project roadmaps, and business credentials as confidential industrial assets. We employ zero-trust access controls, multi-factor authentication, and end-to-end cryptographic encryption across all systems.

1 Introduction & Scope of Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to all services, consulting engagements, software development projects, and web portals operated by Bright Forge Inc. ("Bright Forge", "we", "us", or "our"). This policy governs the manner in which Bright Forge collects, uses, maintains, and discloses information collected from visitors of our website (brightforges.github.io) and enterprise clients utilizing our engineering foundry.

By engaging with our website or entering into a Statement of Work (SOW) with Bright Forge, you acknowledge and agree to the practices outlined in this policy.

2 Information We Collect

We collect information in three primary categories:

  • Direct Identification Details: When you submit a project inquiry, request a blueprint quote, or contact our engineering leads, we collect your full name, business email address, phone number, company name, and project scope requirements.
  • Client Project & Technical Data: When under contract, we may handle architectural diagrams, API endpoints, repository access credentials, and environment parameters strictly required to build and deploy your software.
  • Automated System Telemetry: Log data including IP addresses, browser types, operating systems, referring URLs, and diagnostic session metrics to safeguard against distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) threats.

3 Client Code & Proprietary IP Isolation Guarantee

Our Strict "Zero Unauthorized Training" Policy:

Bright Forge guarantees that no client source code, proprietary algorithms, database schemas, or trade secrets are ever ingested into public artificial intelligence models, third-party LLM training pipelines, or shared repositories. Every client workspace is isolated within dedicated virtual private clouds (VPCs) with strict role-based access control (RBAC).

4 How We Utilize Your Information

We process your data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Engineering, testing, deploying, and maintaining custom software deliverables.
  • Generating architectural estimates, formal project bids, and timeline milestones.
  • Delivering critical security patches, emergency on-call notices, and status alerts.
  • Fulfilling legal, tax, accounting, and SOC 2 / HIPAA compliance audits.

5 Enterprise Security Infrastructure

We implement comprehensive defense-in-depth security measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure:

Cryptographic Encryption

All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3. Data at rest is encrypted via AES-256 with rotating KMS keys.

Zero-Trust Access Control

Hardware MFA tokens, ephemeral SSH credentials, and least-privilege policies enforced across all engineer accounts.

6 Third-Party Cloud Sub-Processors

Bright Forge works with tier-one cloud providers that adhere to ISO 27001, SOC 2, and FedRAMP standards. Our primary sub-processors include:

Sub-Processor Purpose Location
Amazon Web Services (AWS) Cloud compute, isolated VPCs, storage United States / Global
Google Cloud Platform (GCP) Kubernetes clusters, BigQuery analytics United States / Global
Cloudflare Inc. DDoS mitigation, CDN, edge routing Global Edge Network

7 Your Rights Under GDPR & CCPA/CPRA

Depending on your jurisdiction, you retain full statutory rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: You may request a complete export of your personal information held by us.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request correction of outdated or inaccurate personal details.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request complete deletion of your records, subject to regulatory retention laws.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We do not withhold service or penalize any user exercising privacy rights.
