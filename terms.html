2 Foundry Services & Statements of Work (SOW)

Bright Forge provides specialized digital services, including custom software engineering, cloud infrastructure architecture, artificial intelligence and machine learning system development, user experience design, and security compliance audits.

Each engagement is formalized through a project blueprint or Statement of Work that explicitly defines:

  • Deliverable specifications, acceptance criteria, and technical stack.
  • Sprint schedule, milestone deadlines, and demo review checkpoints.
  • Commercial terms, payment triggers, and project team composition.

3 Intellectual Property Rights & Code Ownership

Complete Work-Made-For-Hire Assignment:

Upon final payment of applicable milestone invoices, Bright Forge grants and assigns to Client all right, title, and interest in and to all custom software deliverables, source code, UI designs, databases, and documentation developed uniquely for Client under the applicable SOW. Client owns all copyright and patent rights free of ongoing royalty obligations.

*Note: Standard open-source libraries (e.g. React, Tailwind CSS, Linux, Python packages) incorporated into deliverables remain subject to their respective permissive licenses (e.g., MIT, Apache 2.0).

4 Sprint Cadence & Client Acceptance Period

Our projects operate on structured bi-weekly development sprints. Upon delivery of a milestone build:

  • Review Period: Client has ten (10) business days from milestone delivery to review, test, and provide written notice of defects or milestone approval.
  • Defect Correction: If Client identifies reproducible defects that deviate from agreed specifications, Bright Forge will remediate such defects at no additional cost.
  • Deemed Acceptance: If no written notice of rejection is provided within ten (10) business days, the milestone is deemed accepted and invoiced accordingly.

5 Post-Launch Warranty & SLAs

Bright Forge stands firmly behind the durability of its code. Every custom software deliverable includes:

90-Day Bug Warranty

Immediate remediation of any critical functional bugs or architectural defects reported within 90 days of production launch at zero fee.

Emergency Response SLA

For ongoing retainer clients, guaranteed 1-hour critical response times with 24/7 telemetry monitoring and automated incident escalation.
